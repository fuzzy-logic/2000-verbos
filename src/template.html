What do you want to work on?

Settings

Downloads the real Paraguayan recordings to this device. Once a pack is saved, every speak button plays it instead of a synthetic voice, and the walking track works offline with the screen off. Use wifi.

Listen — read the Spanish while you hear it, glance at the English. Fast, no pressure. This is wave one.

Recall — the Spanish is blurred. Read the English, say it out loud in Spanish, then tap to check. You will get most wrong at first. That is the exercise working, not failing.

The sandwich — once these feel easy, push the speed to 1.25–1.5× and alternate: five minutes of a real Paraguayan podcast, then back here when it gets tiring. Repeat. That gap is where listening comprehension is actually built.
